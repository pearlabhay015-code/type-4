University Statutes & Regulations

Central University of South Bihar is governed by the Central Universities Act, 2009. Official regulations and directives are established by the Executive Council and Academic Council of the university.

  • Code of Academic Conduct: Students must maintain 75% attendance to qualify for examinations.
  • Anti-Ragging Act: Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited on campus. Offenders will face legal action and expulsion.
  • Hostel Code: Inmates must adhere to gate timings (8:00 PM for Boys, 7:00 PM for Girls) for safety.

Web Accessibility Statement

We are committed to making the CUSB web portal accessible to all users, including individuals with visual, auditory, or motor impairments. The site is designed in compliance with World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA.

  • Bilingual rendering supports local and national languages.
  • Font Scaling bar allows sizes from 12px to 22px without breaking layout integrity.
  • Theme Switcher toggle shifts background to pure black for high contrast readability.
