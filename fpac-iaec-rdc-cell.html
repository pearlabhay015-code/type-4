Faculty Project Approval Committee (FPAC)

Any application for a sponsored research project to an external funding agency is routed as a formal institutional proposal. This document details the research aims, deliverables, staff requirement, investigator core competence, budget estimate, and institutional overheads.

All sponsored project proposals are processed through the Project Monitoring & Administration Cell of the University and vetted by the Faculty Project Approval Committee (FPAC) of the respective School before submission.

Constitution of FPAC for an Academic School

  • Dean of the concerned School (Chairperson).
  • Head of the concerned Department / Centre.
  • Two Senior Professors of the concerned School / Department / Centre.
  • One Senior Professor of an allied Academic Department.
  • Principal Investigator (PI) / Co-PI of the concerned research project.

Three Professors as above are nominated by the Vice-Chancellor on a rotational basis annually. Recommended proposals are forwarded to the Deputy Registrar (Academic) for final institutional endorsement.
