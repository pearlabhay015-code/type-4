Faculty Research Funding Ecosystem

Sponsored research grants are an integral driver of academic career advancement, frontier discoveries, and institutional capacity building. Faculty members at the Central University of South Bihar actively secure extramural competitive research grants across scientific, technological, social science, and legal disciplines.

🔬 Extramural Major Projects

Multi-year research grants from apex national bodies including DST, SERB (CRG/SURE/POWER), DBT, CSIR, ICMR, and ICSSR for state-of-the-art laboratory setups.

✈️ Travel & Conference Grants

Institutional and national funding (UGC/INSA/SERB) for presenting peer-reviewed research papers at prestigious international conferences and symposia.
