NOTIFICATION
4th Convocation Communique

The 4th Convocation of Central University of South Bihar will be held in 1st Week of March, 2025 at Central University of South Bihar to award Degree to the students who have passed in the Year - 2021 & 2022. The candidates who have passed the Under Graduate, Post Graduate and Ph.D. Examinations during the aforementioned period and qualified for the award of Degree are hereby informed to visit the CUSB website www.cusb.ac.in and register themselves through Convocation Registration Link (http://cusb.samarth.edu.in/convocation) available at CUSB website latest by 22.02.2025. An amount of Rs.500/- (non-refundable) on account of Convocation fee is required to be paid online for the same.

An amount of Rs.500/- (refundable) is to be paid for the costumes (Pagdi & Angvastra) at time to distribution of costumes which will be refunded on return of the costumes. The date of distribution of costumes shall be intimated later.

All candidates registered for participation in the Convocation must submit receipt of Convocation fee, identity proof, a copy of Aadhaar Card alongwith a copy of last Semester Grade Report / Cumulative Grade Report / Provisional Degree Certificate / Migration certificate issued to them while reporting to collect the costumes.

For any further information, please contact Private Secretary to Controller of Examinations on 0631-2229512 / 2229518 and email to convocation@cusb.ac.in.

Sd/-
