On-campus food service

Madhuban Cafeteria

Madhuban Cafeteria is the named campus cafeteria referenced in the University's current facilities and procurement notices. It is supported by a structured food-service arrangement with an approved menu chart, stated quantities, and set prices for the listed items.

Food-service operations are reviewed by the Cafeteria / Canteen Committee. This page keeps students informed without directing them to any external portal.

Looking for a current item or rate?The applicable item list and prices are displayed through the campus cafeteria menu chart.

Published operating framework

How the cafeteria menu is managed

CUSB's published canteen terms set the framework for the menu and regular performance review. Individual menu choices and prices should be checked at the cafeteria because they are administered through the applicable menu chart.

A

Approved menu chart

The University provides a menu chart covering each listed item's quantity and price.

B

Price consistency

The service provider supplies the items using the approved chart and may not independently fix or change an item's rate.

C

Committee review

The Cafeteria / Canteen Committee reviews performance after each one-year period for any extension decision.

D

assistance

Questions or feedback can be recorded through the University's enquiry route on this website.
