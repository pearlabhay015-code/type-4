Campus Dining

Cafeteria & Mess

Spacious canteen serving hygienic food, beverages, and snacks at government approved subsidized rates.

Hygienic and Diverse Cuisines

The university canteen is designed to cater to the culinary diversity of students joining CUSB from various states across India. Equipped with separate cooking blocks for vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus, it focuses heavily on fresh ingredients and cleanliness.

The dining hall accommodates 150+ students concurrently. In addition to the main cafeteria, dry-canteen points are set up near the academic blocks for quick access to tea, coffee, and light snacks during lecture breaks.

Dining Points on Campus

🍳

Central Canteen

Located near the administrative block. Open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Serving meals, breakfast items, fresh fruit juices, and Chinese appetizers.

☕

Academic Block Kiosks

Quick service counters offering packaged snacks, tea, coffee, and mineral water, operating during academic hours to support students and visitors.

🍎

Co-operative Hostel Mess

Located within Aryabhatta and Gargi hostel blocks. Managed by student committees on a zero-profit basis, ensuring balanced nutritious diets daily.
