💡 Ministry of Education Innovation Cell

Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC)

Fostering a vibrant culture of innovation, ideation, technology startups, and entrepreneurship across the university community.

About Innovation Cell

Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India has established the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) program to systematically foster the culture of innovation amongst all Higher Education Institutions (HEIs). The primary mandate of IIC is to encourage, inspire, and nurture young students by supporting them to work with new ideas and transform them into functional prototypes.

Key Mandates & Functions of IIC

As outlined by the MoE Innovation Cell guidelines, the council executes the following core functions:

  • Conduct innovation and entrepreneurship-related activities prescribed by Central MIC in a time-bound manner.
  • Identify and reward student and faculty innovations, nurturing promising ideas and sharing regional success stories.
  • Organize periodic workshops, seminars, design thinking sessions, and interactive roundtables with venture capitalists, patent attorneys, and founders.
  • Create a mentor pool for student innovators and facilitate networking with national incubation centers and MSME hubs.
  • Establish an institutional innovation portal to showcase research projects, prototypes, and patent filings.
  • Organize internal hackathons, idea pitching challenges, and mini-competitions with direct industry involvement.

Activities & Committee Constitution

The Vice-Chancellor has constituted the institutional IIC Committee comprising senior faculty, innovation activity coordinators, startup mentors, and student council conveners to steer the university's innovation ecosystem.
