Engineering Wing

Engineering section of Central University of South Bihar came into existence from 2016 at Gaya for construction of new buildings under its supervision, attending the repair/maintenance of the University buildings which includes all the buildings of Academics/Administration and residential of the University have been constructed hitherto.

  • Engineering section works on planning on new projects and executes the same on close supervision. Various building and infrastructure related maintenance and allied works are executed by inviting E-tendering, quotations and correspondence with construction agency of Central Government and State Government for execution of building projects, services etc.
  • Civil and Electrical related facility works are undertaken by engineering section after administrative and financial approval by the CA from time to time depending upon the requirement from various departments through requisition form.
  • The water supply to the Academic, Admin-, hostels, Residence of the University campus are maintained through UGT with underground water line networks and similarly the electrical supply of the University buildings/campus are maintained through 1 no. of power 11kV / 433 V substation.
  • University has its own STP (Sewage Treatment Plant) of capacity of 280 KLD to cater irrigation water and flush water
  • Engineering section prepares agendas and minutes for building works committee and provides data to the ministry related to buildings and services time to time.
Presently, a new work has been awarded for construction of Library Building, Hostels, Staff Quarters, Health Centre, Animal House, Food Court etc. is going on.
