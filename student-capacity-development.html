Soft Skill

Soft Skills require a great deal of attention for the students of all disciplines seeking better career prospects. Considering the need to improve Soft Skills of students for enduring success, Central University of South Bihar has emphasized to conduct Soft Skills trainings programmes on the campus at regular intervals.

Also, in an endeavor to remove the skills gaps and with the objective of creating market-ready professionals, Central University of South Bihar has embedded Soft Skills Courses within the academic curriculum for all the Students across the programme of studies to train them for their better professional and personal life.

The key focus areas of the soft skills trainings include:

  • Overall Personality Development
  • Interview skills and techniques
  • Stress management
  • Meditation
  • Goal setting and time Management
  • Leadership Development
  • Communication skills
  • Presentation skills
  • Computer Awareness
  • Creative Thinking
  • Physical fitness
  • Inter-personal skills
  • Professional Etiquettes
  • Team Building
  • Adaptability
