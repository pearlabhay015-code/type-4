Auditorium

The Seminar Hall Complex has a Swami Vivekanad Lecture Hall (on the 2nd floor), which is fully air-conditioned and has a seating capacity of 150 persons. It also has high-quality projection system and smart classroom facility. In addition, there are three halls with a seating capacity of 60 each on the same floor to organize parallel sessions or meetings. These facilities are made available on prescribed rent to the organizers of events from other faculties in the university, and also to other organizations for holding academic events.

150 Seats Swami Vivekanand Main Hall
3 Halls 60 Capacity Each (Parallel Sessions)
