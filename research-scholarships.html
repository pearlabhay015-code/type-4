Institutional Scholarship Schemes

The Central University of South Bihar has instituted diverse scholarship programmes to ensure that financial hardship never hinders academic excellence. The University Scholarship Cell oversees the transparent execution, monitoring, and disbursement of institutional scholarships and national fellowships.

🎓

Merit-cum-Means Scholarship

Tuition fee waiver for up to 20% of enrolled students in each academic programme, supporting high-performing students who require financial assistance.

💼

Earn While You Learn

Students may be assigned departmental or library technical tasks up to 10 hours per week, fostering self-reliance, practical skills, and leadership.

⭐

Attendance Based Merit Award

Students achieving 100% attendance in a semester receive academic books of their choice or direct reimbursement of up to ₹1,000 per semester.

🏆

Entrance & Semester Toppers

Dedicated financial reward and citation for students securing first rank in national entrance examinations (CUET) or end-semester assessments.

♿

Scholarship to Divyang Students

Special financial assistance and monthly transport subsidies for full-time Divyang students to ensure barrier-free mobility across campus.

🤝

Azim Premji Foundation Fellowship

External philanthropic scholarship opportunities and stipend guidance for education, development, and social science scholars.
