Institution Ethics Committee for Biomedical and Health Research (IECBHR)

Any research institution conducting biomedical and health research involving human participants, tissues, or bio-samples is mandated to constitute an Ethics Committee under the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019 and the National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research Involving Human Participants formulated by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR).

The primary purpose of the IECBHR is to safeguard the dignity, rights, safety, confidentiality, and well-being of all research participants. CUSB has constituted an active Ethics Committee registered with the Department of Health Research (DHR), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India.

Scope & Ethical Review Process

The committee reviews all research protocols involving:

  • Biomedical, physiological, immunological, and epidemiological studies in humans.
  • Socio-behavioural and psychological inquiries involving patient data, interviews, or mental health surveys.
  • Recombinant DNA (rDNA) technology, genetic modifications, and biological containment under IBSC.
  • Verification of informed consent forms, patient information sheets, and safety disclosure routines.
