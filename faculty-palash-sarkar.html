Dr. Palash Sarkar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the Central University of South Bihar. His core research focuses on Combinatorics, Sequence and Code Design for Wireless Communications, theoretical bounds on set size and correlation magnitude for codebooks, and algebraic structures including Boolean and Bent functions over rings and graphs for sequence construction in next-generation communications (NOMA, MC-CDMA, OFDM, Radar). He has held prestigious postdoctoral research fellowships at the University of Bergen, Norway and IIT Patna.

🔬 Research Interests

Core Specialization

Sequence & Code Design for Wireless Communications

Design and construction of Complementary Sequences, Quasi-Complementary Sequence Sets (QCSS), Zero Correlation Zone (ZCZ/ZCP) sequences, Complete Complementary Codes (CCC), and Golay Complementary Sets for modern multi-carrier communication systems.

Pure & Applied Math

Combinatorics & Boolean Functions

Investigating Generalized Boolean Functions, Bent functions over rings and graphs, Pseudo-Boolean functions, and Generalized Reed-Muller codes for optimal algebraic sequence generation.

Theoretical Bounds

Bounds on Set Size & Correlation Magnitude

Determining mathematical and theoretical limits on correlation magnitudes, codebook capacities, and Peak-to-Mean Envelope Power Ratios (PMEPR) for wireless signal processing.

Applications

NOMA, MC-CDMA, OFDM & Radar Systems

Practical applications of codebooks in Non-Orthogonal Multiple Access (NOMA), Multi-Carrier CDMA, Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM), and radar waveform synthesis.
