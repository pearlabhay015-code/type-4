About the library

Central Library

The Central Library is a key division of CUSB supporting the University’s academic, research, training and consultancy programmes. Its print and electronic collections, modern information technology and designed study spaces support the information needs of students, researchers, faculty and other users.

The Library operates from the third floor of the Administrative Building and provides managed academic-resource information alongside its physical services.

Library Sections

Services and sections

Circulation Section

The entry-point service desk supports lending, book availability enquiries, fines, membership forms, no-dues certificates and usage records. The RFID-enabled library supports self-issue through KIOSK and return through the drop box.

Stack Section

Books are arranged in Dewey Decimal Classification order, from 000 to 999. The Library follows an open-access system with bay guides to help users find material.

Reference Section

Reference material includes encyclopaedias, dictionaries, bibliographies, indexes, atlases, government publications, reports, theses and dissertations, journals, newspapers and magazines for in-library consultation.

Binding Section

The Binding Section repairs damaged reading material, old journals, rare books and reference print resources to extend their usable life.

Central Library Office

The office manages routine administration, correspondence, invoice processing, activity records and resource requirements.

Acquisition Section

This section coordinates recommended book lists, duplicate checking, purchase-committee processing, supply orders, verification and accessioning of approved resources.

Technical Section

Technical processing includes classification, cataloguing, subject headings, transcription, barcoding, RFID tagging and release for circulation.

Reading Section

The Reading Section provides access to books, periodicals and newspapers for users of the Library’s collection.

Upcoming Sections

The Library identifies Online Resource and Braille sections as developing services for its users.

Circulation

Borrowing and circulation service

The Central Library was automated with KOHA and barcode technology in 2011; RFID technology and KOHA were implemented in 2020. Users can search books through Web OPAC, receive circulation assistance at the desk and use RFID self-service facilities.

Borrowing access

Faculty, officers, research scholars, UG/PG students, non-teaching staff and inter-library-loan users have borrowing entitlements according to their patron category and item type.

Returns and renewal

Renewal must be completed on or before the due date. Student stack books are not renewed, though they may be borrowed again where no reservation has been placed.

Fine and replacement

Overdue charges for general-shelf books are Rs. 1 per book per day. Lost or unusably damaged material is settled by replacement or the applicable Library charge; no-dues certificates are issued after dues are cleared.

Inter-library loan

General-shelf/stack books may be borrowed. Journals, current magazines/newspapers, theses, dissertations, reference works and audio-visual media are for in-library use and are not issued.

E-Resources

E-books, journals and databases

E-book collection

The Library reports 6,400+ subscribed e-books across disciplines including life sciences, chemistry, commerce, computer science, education, languages, earth and environmental sciences, law, mathematics, pharmacy, physics, psychology and sociological studies.

E-databases

Five named databases support Law & Governance and multidisciplinary study: Manupatra Online, SCC Online, HeinOnline, DELNET and ISID. The Library also lists e-ShodhSindhu resources including Ouriginal, World e-Book Library and South Asia Archive.
