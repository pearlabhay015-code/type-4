About the Central Library

The Central Library began functioning in 2009 when the University campus was temporarily housed at BIT Patna. It is now a focal academic resource for students, researchers, faculty and staff at CUSB, Gaya.

The Library is fully automated with KOHA and RFID technology. Users can search the collection through Web OPAC, issue books through biometric enabled self-service KIOSK, return books through the RFID drop box, and receive circulation notifications.

The official library records also note remote access to subscribed e-books, e-journals and databases through a single platform, plus CCTV surveillance, dedicated library servers, air-conditioned reading/reference areas and expanding reading spaces.

Sections and Services

Circulation Section

Handles front desk service, membership, issue/return support, no-dues certificates, user statistics, self-checkout support and OPAC guidance.

Stack Section

Maintains books arranged by Dewey Decimal Classification from 000 to 999, with open access shelves and bay guides.

Reference Section

Provides encyclopedias, dictionaries, bibliographies, indexes, atlases, government publications, reports, theses, dissertations, journals, newspapers and magazines.

Acquisition Section

Coordinates departmental book recommendations, duplicate checking, purchase committee approval, supply orders, verification and accessioning.

Technical Section

Classifies, catalogues, assigns subject headings, barcodes, RFID tags and prepares resources for circulation.

Binding Section

Repairs damaged books, rare material, old journals and reference resources to increase their usable life.

Research Support Service

Supports researchers with learning material, inter-library loan, reprography, e-books, e-journals, e-databases and MyLoft remote access.

Reprography Service

Photocopying follows copyright rules. Only up to 20 percent of a document may be photocopied; cover-to-cover photocopying is prohibited.

Rules & Borrowing Entitlement

  • Only registered CUSB students, research fellows, faculty and staff are authorized to use Central Library services.
  • Valid ID card is required for entry; silence, decorum and discipline must be maintained.
  • Cell phones are not allowed inside the library.
  • Reference books, printed journals and current/previous journal volumes are non-issuable and must be used in the library.
  • Personal books, bags, umbrellas, folders and water bottles must not be brought inside; laptops and stationery may be used.
  • No-dues certificate is issued only after all borrowed books are returned and dues are cleared.

Research Corners

INFLIBNET Research Corner & RJCL Corner

Rajarshi Janak Central Library established the INFLIBNET Research Corner and RJCL Corner to strengthen research support and scholarly engagement. The corners were inaugurated by the Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor, Prof. K. N. Singh.

The INFLIBNET Research Corner supports awareness and guided use of e-ShodhSindhu, Shodhganga, Shodhgangotri, IRINS, VIDWAN and related platforms. The RJCL Corner showcases university publications, faculty research outputs and academic achievements.
