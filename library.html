Central Library

Empowering scholars and students with 80,000+ volumes, 15,000+ electronic resources, and specialized research facilities.

Knowledge Powerhouse

The Central Library of the Central University of South Bihar is a fully automated knowledge center, utilizing the SOUL database management system for seamless cataloging and circulation services. It provides access to high-quality print and online databases supporting interdisciplinary studies.

Equipped with an e-resource browsing zone, visual reading aids for visually impaired students, and spacious reading halls, the library fosters a quiet, learning-focused environment for over 3,000 active students and researchers.

Library Divisions & Services

📖

Circulation Division

Handles book lending, renewals, returns, and library membership registration using barcode reader technologies.

🖥️

E-Library & Browse Zone

40+ high-speed computer terminals dedicated to research scholars and students to browse e-databases, journals, and thesis repositories.

👓

Accessibility Assist

Specialized reading stations equipped with Braille printers, NVDA screen readers, and visual magnifier monitors for visually impaired students.

🏫

Reference & Periodicals

Hosts rare books, dictionaries, encyclopedias, and current issues of prints journals, magazines, and newspapers in reading rooms.
