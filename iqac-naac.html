'A++' Grade Accreditation with CGPA 3.58

Central University of South Bihar has achieved the highest standard of academic, pedagogical, and administrative excellence, accredited with Grade A++ (CGPA 3.58 on a 4-point scale) by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).

Quality Framework & Seven Assessment Criteria

The assessment process by NAAC evaluates institutions across seven rigorous criteria: Curricular Aspects, Teaching-Learning and Evaluation, Research and Innovations, Infrastructure and Learning Resources, Student Support and Progression, Governance and Leadership, and Institutional Values and Best Practices. The IQAC cell coordinates all continuous internal quality mechanisms ensuring ongoing enhancement across each criterion.
