Visit Bharat Youth Parliament

About

The National Youth Parliament Festival has been reimagined as into the Viksit Bharat Youth Parliament, a platform for young minds to contribute to the vision of Viksit Bharat @2047, as envisioned by Hon'ble Prime Minister. This is a unique opportunity for youth to express their perspectives and ideas on the future of our nation.

How to Participate:

  • Registration: To participate in the Viksit Bharat Youth Parliament, please register your profile on the MY Bharat Portal by clicking on the 'APPLY NOW' button.
  • Video Submission: Following profile completion, upload a one-minute video on the platform addressing the topic: "What does Viksit Bharat mean to you?" This video is crucial for your eligibility in the District Nodal Round.
  • Districts Covered: This nodal center covers the following district: Aurangabad, Nawada, Jehanabad, Gaya
  • Registration Deadline: Registration closes on March 9th at 11:59 PM.
