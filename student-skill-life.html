Life Skills (Yoga, Physical Fitness, Health And Hygiene)

Life Skills Training is designed to make the students physically and mentally fit to lead their day-to-day life more effectively and efficiently and to train themselves in the competitive and stressful environment. It enables the students to understand the significance of a healthy mind and body to lead a fruitful and successful life. The Life Skill trainings include the following Programmes:

  • Yoga
  • Physical fitness
  • Physical Health
  • Mental Health
  • Health and Hygiene Programs
