Awareness Of Trend Technology

Knowledge of technology allows a student access to widely available information and research. Moreover, various job opportunities are technology-specific and a student needs to develop these skills to keep in tune with the fast age of technology growth. The COVID-19 pandemic significantly shifted education from traditional to an online version, which was an emergent state for teachers and students.

Moreover, the effective integration of ICT is essential in systematizing an efficient educational programs in online mode. The successful application of ICT not only contributes to learners' satisfaction but also helps individuals to acquire their desired outcomes. It is, therefore, essential to develop competencies in teachers to use ICT effectively in their pedagogical practices by organizing professional development programs.

The substantive situation thus raises the importance of technology integration in education, and teachers and students are required to update their competencies, respectively.

Programs are organized frequently to underline the importance of staying updated and to help students learn technology skills. Students actively participate in these workshops, seminars, conferences, software training, and tech events.
