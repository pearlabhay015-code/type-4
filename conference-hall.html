Conference Hall

The University Conference Hall is a state-of-the-art executive meeting and symposium facility equipped with premium acoustic wooden paneling, motorized HD smart display, dedicated delegate microphone systems, high-speed Wi-Fi, and climate-controlled central air conditioning. Designed to host Executive Council meetings, Academic Council sessions, international symposiums, press conferences, and high-level academic deliberations.
