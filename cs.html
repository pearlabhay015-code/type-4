Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science at CUSB is committed to research-led teaching in foundational and applied computing. With a curriculum aligned to NEP 2020 and industry requirements, students gain expertise in algorithms, artificial intelligence, data science, and software engineering — preparing them for careers in technology and research across India and globally.

Seats: 25
Entrance: CUET (UG)
