Sports & Gym

Promoting healthy active lifestyles through multi-sport fields, athletics tracks, and fully equipped fitness centers.

Lead an Active Campus Life

At Central University of South Bihar, physical health is prioritized alongside academic rigor. The university sports infrastructure spans across multiple acres, featuring specialized courts and outdoor fields designed to train student athletes and host regional inter-university championships.

The Sports Complex includes a state-of-the-art Indoor Arena for table tennis, carrom, and chess, while the outdoor grounds support high-energy disciplines like football, cricket, volleyball, and basketball. The fitness gym is equipped with cardiovascular and strength-training equipment accessible to all hostel boarders.

CUSB Khel Mahotsav

The Khel Mahotsav is the university's annual athletic meet that brings together teams from all departments to compete in track events, football, cricket, and indoor challenges. It serves as a selection trials for representation at East Zone and National levels.
