Sport at CUSB

A campus built for participation

The Games and Sports Committee promotes competitive sport, physical fitness, and student wellbeing. It manages the campus's outdoor fields and indoor facilities, supports university teams, and conducts activities across the University.

The Milkha Singh Sports Complex provides the main outdoor venue, with facilities for practice, competition, and day-night events.

Access priorityBonafide students and staff may use the Sports Complex. University Sports Team members receive preference when facilities are in use.
Milkha Singh Sports Complex

Outdoor facilities

The main open-air complex combines athletics, team-sport grounds, competition support areas, and essential facilities for participants and spectators.

01

Track & courts

400-metre athletics track; two basketball courts, including one synthetic and one hard court; and two volleyball courts, including one synthetic court.

02

Field sports

Cricket, football, hockey, handball, kabaddi, and kho-kho facilities support a broad range of outdoor sports.

03

Competition support

High-mast lighting, a viewers' gallery, prize-distribution stage, games and sports equipment store, and first-aid facility support events.

04

Player amenities

Separate changing rooms and washrooms are provided for boys and girls at the complex.

Indoor sport and wellbeing

Year-round places to train and reset

Alongside the outdoor complex, CUSB provides indoor games, fitness, yoga, and meditation spaces across the campus.

5

Hostel badminton courts

Three are located in the boys' hostel and two in the girls' hostel.

4

Table-tennis courts

Two are in the boys' hostel, one in the girls' hostel, and one in the Department of Physical Education at Malviya Bhawan.

Gymnasium

Fitness at Malviya Bhawan

A gymnasium with modern workout equipment is available at Malviya Bhawan.

50

Yoga Hall capacity

The Yoga Hall at Malviya Bhawan can accommodate 50 participants in a session; a meditation centre is also located inside the Stupa.

Use the complex responsibly

  • Maintain discipline, decorum, and cleanliness.
  • Use facilities only with proper authorization where required.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, pets, arms, and ammunition are prohibited.
  • Photography or videography needs prior approval.
  • Park only in designated areas; vehicles are not allowed inside unless permitted.
