International Student

International student information

The Indian Council for Cultural Relations supports international cultural exchange and educational cooperation. Its scholarship programmes help overseas students pursue higher education in India and encourage mutual understanding between India and partner countries.

International applicants should use the local help desk for guidance on the programme documents and application process, then follow the admission instructions applicable to their programme.
