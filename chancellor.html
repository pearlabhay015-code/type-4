Hon'ble Chancellor

Dr. C. P. Thakur

M.D., M.R.C.P., F.R.C.P. (London) · Eminent Physician & Former Union Minister

Dr. C. P. Thakur is the Hon'ble Chancellor of the Central University of South Bihar. A world-renowned physician, medical researcher, and statesman, he has made monumental contributions to medical sciences and higher education across India.

🔬 Medical Breakthroughs & Kala-Azar Research

Dr. C. P. Thakur is globally celebrated for his pioneering biomedical contribution in finding cure protocols and affordable medication for Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis / black fever), a life-threatening neglected tropical disease that affected millions across Bihar and South Asia.

His seminal research revolutionized tropical medicine therapeutics, earning recognition from the World Health Organization (WHO) and international medical bodies. Over his distinguished research career, he has authored over 300 high-impact research publications in prestigious international peer-reviewed journals.

🏛️ Ministerial & Parliamentary Leadership

Dr. Thakur served with high distinction in the Union Cabinet as the Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare from 2000 to 2002, and as Union Minister of Small Scale Industries in the Government of India.

During his ministerial tenure, he introduced progressive public healthcare schemes, strengthened national disease surveillance networks, expanded AIIMS infrastructure across India, and formulated health policies targeting communicable disease eradication.

🎓 Academic Qualifications & Fellowships

  • M.D. (Medicine) — Top Academic Honors in Clinical Medicine.
  • M.R.C.P. (London) — Member of the Royal College of Physicians, United Kingdom.
  • F.R.C.P. (London) — Fellow of the Royal College of Physicians, London.
  • Conferred numerous Lifetime Achievement Awards by national and international medical academies.

🌟 Vision for Central University of South Bihar

As Chancellor of CUSB, Dr. C. P. Thakur guides the institution towards global academic excellence, interdisciplinary research, modern scientific innovation, and community-centered education that empowers youth and revives Bihar's ancient legacy as a global seat of learning.
