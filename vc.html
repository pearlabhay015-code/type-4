Vice-Chancellor

Prof. Kameshwar Nath Singh

Vice-Chancellor, Central University of South Bihar, Gaya

Prof. Kameshwar Nath Singh is the 3rd Vice-Chancellor of Central University of South Bihar (CUSB), Gaya. A distinguished geographer, institution-builder, and educational visionary, Prof. Singh brings over 32 years of rich professional experience across teaching, advanced research, international project leadership, and executive university administration.

🎓 Academic Foundations & Prior Vice-Chancellorships

Prior to assuming leadership at CUSB, Prof. Singh served as the Vice-Chancellor of Uttar Pradesh Rajarshi Tandon Open University, Prayagraj and Prof. Rajendra Singh (Rajju Bhaiya) State University, Prayagraj.

He completed his graduation, post-graduation, and Ph.D. from Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University, Gorakhpur. He commenced his academic career at the same University, where he served as Professor & Head of the Department of Geography and was the Founder Director of the Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Research Foundation. He has supervised 20 Ph.D. scholars and one post-doctoral fellow.

🌍 International Research & DAAD Fellowship (Germany)

Prof. Singh worked as a Research Associate at the Institute of Geography and Institute for Development Research and Development Policy at Ruhr University, Bochum, Germany under a joint research project sponsored by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD).

He conducted extensive scientific field surveys across Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Luxembourg under the mentorship of Late Prof. K. H. Hottes. His scientific contributions focus on environmental sustainability, regional economic planning, and value-based education.

📚 Books, Publications & Editorial Leadership

  • Author of 17 academic books, including the benchmark volume ‘Geography of India: Yesterday, Today & Tomorrow’.
  • More than 70 research and policy papers published in indexed journals.
  • President of the Deccan Geographical Society of India, Pune and President of the Association of North Indian Geographers.
  • Executive Editor of the ‘Journal of Eco-development’ and ‘Uttar Bharat Bhugol Patrika’.
  • Founder General Secretary of the Institute for Rural Eco-development.

🏛️ National Statutory Roles & Honors

Prof. Singh serves as the President of India / Visitor’s Nominee on the Executive Council of Central University of Jharkhand, Ranchi, Governor’s Nominee on the Executive Council of the University of Allahabad, Senate Member at IIT Patna, and Esteemed Member of the Collegium of Eminent Social Scientists constituted by ICSSR, New Delhi.

He has been honoured with the Best Geographer Award (2014) by the Deccan Geographical Society, Distinguished Citizen Award, and State Award for excellence in National Service Scheme coordination.

🎯 Vision for CUSB: NEP 2020 & AatmaNirbhar Bharat

As Vice-Chancellor of CUSB, Prof. K. N. Singh has prioritized establishing a congenial learning environment, transparent and paperless e-governance (Samarth ERP), advanced multidisciplinary research labs, and robust student support systems aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020). Grounded in the philosophy of Integral Humanism, ‘Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam’, and ‘Vocal for Local’, he is steering CUSB towards becoming an internationally acclaimed centre of academic and innovation excellence.
