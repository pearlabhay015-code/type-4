Quick Answers (FAQ)

How to apply for CUSB Admissions 2026?

Admissions are conducted through the National Testing Agency (NTA) CUET examination. Visit cuet.samarth.ac.in to apply.

What is the hostel fee structure?

Hostel rent is approximately ₹3,500 per semester plus mess charges. Detailed breakdown is available on the Hostel Portal page.

Are scholarships available for SC/ST/OBC?

Yes! CUSB supports National Scholarship Portal (NSP), Bihar Post-Matric, and university merit fee exemptions.
