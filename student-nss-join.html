Join NSS ( Registration For 2024-25 )

The overall aim of National Service Scheme (NSS) is to give an extra dimension to the higher education system and orient the student youth to community service while studying in educational institution. Being an educated youth, most of you are expected to take the reins of administration in future, however, due to lack of the awareness to the problems of the village/slum and/or community, many a times, you will not be able to help the society in a way which is required for proper shaping up the need of common people and/or community. Thus, for arousing the social conscience within you, and to provide an opportunity to work with the people in the villages/slums and to expose you to the realities of life, NSS is working with full dedication and passion. Moreover, it would also help in bringing about a change in your social perception. You as a volunteer of National Service Scheme (NSS), your objective would be to

  1. Understand the community in which you work
  2. Understand yourself in relation to your community;
  3. Identify the needs and problems of the community and involve you in problem solving process;
  4. Develop among themselves a sense of social and civic responsibility;
  5. Utilize your knowledge in finding practical solution to individual and community problems;
  6. Develop competence required for group living and sharing of responsibilities;
  7. Gain skills in mobilizing community participation;
  8. Acquire leadership qualities and democratic attitude;

In this regards, with an immense pleasure, I would like to invite you to join CUSB NSS Unit. The registration drive is open for all. With the NSS philosophy "NOT ME BUT YOU", the NSS unit of Central University of South Bihar will give you the platform where you can hone your skill not just academically but also use your potential to act as a catalyst in serving the society as a whole.

For availing this opportunity kindly click here to Join NSS. After scrutinizing the registered candidates, the concerned NSS committee will notify you about your selection for NSS. After that you need to submit the offline form in the NSS office of the University for getting all necessary stuff to work as a NSS volunteer
