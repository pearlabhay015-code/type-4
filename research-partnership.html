Academic & Industrial Partnerships

The Central University of South Bihar actively fosters strategic partnerships and institutional linkages with universities, research laboratories, hospitals, and industrial enterprises in India and abroad for mutual academic growth and research excellence. CUSB has executed multiple Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) across medical, physical, biological, social, and legal sciences, with several international agreements in progress.

Procedure for Signing an MoU with CUSB

The university follows a structured institutional vetting process for finalizing collaborative agreements:

Step 01

Identification

Mutual areas of academic cooperation and research scope are outlined by participating departments.

Step 02

Drafting

Draft agreement is formulated based on the standard CUSB MoU framework terms.

Step 03

Legal Vetting

Submitted to the University Legal Cell for statutory review and compliance verification.

Step 04

Approval & Signing

Placed before Academic Council / Executive Council for final endorsement and signing.
