Hostel Facilities

CUSB provides premium separate hostel facilities for boys and girls within our secure 300-acre green campus.

Hostel Accommodation

Gargi Sadan & Maitreyi Sadan

The girls hostels provide comfortable and safe lodging for postgraduate and research students. Managed by a dedicated female warden team, it boasts robust security measures, a modern common room with a TV, daily newspaper service, and clean dining services.

Aryabhatta Sadan & Malviya Sadan

The boys hostels offer secure, spacious, and student-friendly accommodation. The rooms are double-sharing, well-ventilated, and furnished with a bed, table, and wardrobe. Facilities include an outdoor playground, high-speed Wi-Fi, indoor games, and clean drinking water units.
