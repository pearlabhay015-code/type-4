Residential life at CUSB

Hostel accommodation

Hostel allotment is administered under the University's rules and regulations governing hostel administration and residency. The residence programme is designed to support study, research, mutual respect, and participation in campus life.

Read the Hostel Manual ↗
Girls' Hostels

Maitreyi Sadan & Gargi Sadan

Two blocks with a separate mess building and open space serve more than 300 students. Rooms are provided with a cot and bed, study table, chair, and wardrobe.

  • Reading and recreation rooms with television and indoor games
  • Badminton and volleyball courts, plus fitness facilities
  • Dedicated hostel office, female security guards, and support staff
Boys' Hostel

V. D. Savarkar Hostel — Blocks A & B

Two residential blocks with a separate mess building and open space accommodate more than 550 students. Each room is furnished with a cot and bed, table, chair, and wardrobe.

  • Reading, recreation, visitor, and medical rooms
  • Badminton courts and two gym / fitness centres
  • Dedicated hostel office, security, and residential support staff

Facilities that support daily life

The hostel facilities bring together accommodation, study support, recreation, and a managed residential environment.

01

Furnished stay

Residence rooms are equipped with the essential furniture students need for study and rest.

02

Study & connectivity

Reading spaces and campus Wi-Fi connectivity support study and research beyond class hours.

03

Sport & recreation

Courts, gyms, indoor games, television rooms, and open spaces create room to recharge.

04

Managed support

The residential administration includes wardens, attendants, security personnel, and a dedicated office.
