Hostel Accommodation & Residential Life

Central University of South Bihar provides modern on-campus residential facilities for undergraduate, postgraduate, and Ph.D. students with 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, hygienic dining mess, gymnasiums, and study halls.

Boys' Residential Life

V. D. Savarkar Boys' Hostel

Blocks A & B accommodating 550+ students with 2 fitness gyms, reading rooms, mess, outdoor courts, and residential warden administration.
