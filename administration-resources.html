Leadership

University leadership

Visitor

Her Excellency Smt. Droupadi Murmu

Hon’ble President of India and Visitor of CUSB. She was sworn in as the 15th President of India on 25 July 2022, after serving as Governor of Jharkhand from 2015 to 2021.

Profile

Born at Uparbeda village, Mayurbhanj, Odisha, Smt. Murmu earned a Bachelor of Arts from Ramadevi Women’s College, Bhubaneswar. Her public service has focused on empowering marginalised sections and deepening democratic values.

Chancellor

Dr. C. P. Thakur

M.D., M.R.C.P., F.R.C.P. (London). Dr. C. P. Thakur is a renowned physician known for his contribution to the treatment of Kala azar (visceral leishmaniasis).

He served as Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, from 2000 to 2002 and has published about 300 research articles in medical and clinical sciences.

Vice-Chancellor

Prof. Kameshwar Nath Singh

Prof. Singh is the third Vice-Chancellor of CUSB. Prior to joining the University, he served as Vice-Chancellor of Uttar Pradesh Rajarshi Tandon Open University and Prof. Rajendra Singh (Rajju Bhaiya) State University, Prayagraj.

He has more than 32 years of experience in teaching, research, administration, training and major research projects, including work at Ruhr University, Bochum, Germany.

Student welfare

Prof. Pawan Kumar Mishra

Dean of Student Welfare and Professor, Department of Law and Governance. The Office of the DSW coordinates student welfare, social and cultural activities, counselling, financial aid, scholarships and inclusive support services.

Campus discipline

Proctorial Board

The Proctorial Board ensures observance of rules and regulations, monitors the disciplinary climate on campus and takes preventive or immediate action when required.

Current Proctor: Prof. Pranav Kumar, Professor and Head, Department of Political Science.

Academic leadership

Dean of the School

Deans lead the University’s Schools of Earth, Biological & Environmental Sciences; Mathematics, Statistics & Computer Science; Physical & Chemical Sciences; Social Sciences & Policy; Human Science; Languages & Literature; Media, Arts & Aesthetics; Law & Governance and other Schools.

Registrar

Prof. Narendra Kumar Rana

Prof. Rana is the 8th Registrar of CUSB. He joined on deputation from the Department of Geography, Institute of Science, Banaras Hindu University, where he has been Professor since 2021.

His research interests include fluvial geomorphology, natural resource management, environmental hazards and disaster risk reduction.
