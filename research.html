Research ecosystem

Research at CUSB

CUSB is a research-cum-teaching university where faculty undertake basic, applied and cross-cutting research. The University records support from agencies including DBT, DST, JSPS, SERB, UNEP, UGC, ICMR, ICSSR, BRNS and MoEF.

Its research ecosystem brings biological, physical, chemical, computational, earth, environmental, social and human sciences together to address societal problems and sustainable development goals.

Central Instrumental Facility

Central Instrumental Facility

Established in 2019 with departmental and University support, CIF provides advanced analytical instruments for multidisciplinary research. It is operated by qualified faculty and technical staff and is open to researchers from universities, academic and national research institutes, private institutes and industry.

Vision, mission and objectives

Vision: provide advanced analytical techniques for cutting-edge science and technology research. Mission: facilitate researchers with modern scientific technology. Its objectives include data-acquisition guidance, instrument workshops and access to sophisticated analytical equipment for R&D measurement.

Facility and Services

Media Studio

The soundproof electronic media production lab includes Sony 4K professional cameras, audio console, video mixer, talkback, virtual chroma and real sets, an Apple Mac-Pro editing suite, teleprompter, live-streaming equipment and editing/graphics bays.

Available equipment

Teleprompter; three-camera studio setup; touch-screen computer; interactive board; virtual reality system; character generator; AUX camera; video switcher; audio mixer; multiview monitor; final window; audio delay unit; talkback; dimmer light; light controller; chroma set; three-point lighting; and DSLR.
