Advanced Instrumentation Hub

The Central Instruments Facility (CIF) at the Central University of South Bihar serves as the focal point for cutting-edge scientific research. Established to foster interdisciplinary research collaborations, the facility houses sophisticated instruments that are vital for characterization, imaging, structural analysis, and molecular investigations.

Our services are open to internal students, research scholars, and faculty members, as well as external researchers from academic institutions, national laboratories, and private R&D industries on a subsidized fee-per-sample basis.

NABL Aligned Practices

The laboratory aligns with National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) safety guidelines and standardization practices. Sample preparation protocols and data analysis routines are overseen by qualified scientific technicians.

Instrument Booking Process

Follow these steps to book instrument slots and submit samples for analysis.

Step 1: Download Requisition Form

Download the specific instrument requisition form from our Downloads section. Indicate chemical safety hazards and solubility info.

Step 2: Pay Testing Fees

Pay online via the CUSB Samarth Payment Gateway. Retain the e-receipt representing matching testing slots.

Step 3: Sample Submission

Submit samples physically or mail them to the CIF Coordinator along with the form and payment receipt. Results are shared via official email.
