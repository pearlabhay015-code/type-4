University Guest House

The University Guest House (Sangharam) has 03 VIP Rooms (suite room), 32 fully furnished AC rooms, 1 six bed dormitory, 25 seater conference hall and around 36-seater Dining Hall. The Guest House is offering the accommodation facility to all Delegates, Parents/Guardians of the Students residing in Hostel / Alumni /Guest from other academic institutes/relatives of university employees. This Guest House is within the reach of ATM and Bank. The standard operating procedure (sop) for guest house booking is available on the website of the University.
