Comprehensive Medical Care

The Health & Medical Center at the Central University of South Bihar is a dedicated on-campus clinic that serves the healthcare needs of students, research scholars, faculty, and administrative staff. Equipped with outpatient consultation rooms, observation beds, and essential diagnostic devices, it ensures a healthy campus environment.

The clinic provides free consultations, primary dressings, routine health monitoring (blood pressure, glucose checkups), and basic pharmacy distributions. A dedicated 24/7 on-campus ambulance is maintained for high-priority emergency transports to specialized hospitals in Gaya.

Observation Wards

💊

Dispensing Pharmacy

Stocks essential medicines, antibiotics, emergency fluids, and first-aid kits distributed free-of-cost against resident doctor prescriptions.

🧪

Pathology & Diagnostics

Equipped with ECG machines, nebulizers, blood sugar checkers, and testing kits for rapid diagnosis of seasonal fevers and minor ailments.
