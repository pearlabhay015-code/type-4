About the University

Collective Reasoning, National Purpose

Central University of South Bihar (CUSB) is one of the premier central universities established under the Central Universities Act, 2009. Operating from a permanent 300-acre green campus at Panchanpur, Gaya, the university acts as a beacon of high quality higher education and advanced research in the historic state of Bihar.
