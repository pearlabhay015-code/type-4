Bidding Guidelines & Terms

Submission Instructions

Bids must be submitted strictly through the Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP) or in sealed envelopes addressed to the Registrar / Store Officer as specified in the tender document before the stipulated closing time.

EMD & Exemption

Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) should be paid via Demand Draft or Bank Guarantee in favor of "Central University of South Bihar". MSME / NSIC registered firms are exempt as per GOI norms.
