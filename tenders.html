General Procurement & Bidding Guidelines

Essential instructions for bidders, EMD exemption norms, and submission procedures

📌 CPPP Submission Protocol

All bids must be submitted online through the Central Public Procurement Portal (https://eprocure.gov.in/cppp/) or sealed hard copies submitted to Store Officer, CUSB Gaya before the specified deadline.

🛡️ EMD & MSME Exemption Rules

Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) should be drawn in favor of 'Central University of South Bihar'. Micro & Small Enterprises (MSMEs) registered with NSIC/Udyam are exempted from EMD payment as per Govt. of India rules.
