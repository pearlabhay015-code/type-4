Explore CUSB's permanent 300-acre environment equipped with state-of-the-art labs, libraries, residential spaces, and health centers.

World-Class Infrastructure

Central University of South Bihar provides a comprehensive learning ecosytem that supports students in academic work, research, fitness, wellness, and healthy residential life.

Central Library

A knowledge powerhouse holding over 80,000+ volumes, 100+ print journals, and e-resources through national consortia like ShodhSindhu. Fully automated using SOUL database management.

  • Timing: 9:00 AM — 6:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)
  • E-Library: Over 15,000+ online journals available 24/7
  • Special Facilities: Blind and visually impaired reading stations
Explore Central Library

Campus Hostels

Fully furnished separate secure hostels for boys (Aryabhatta & Malviya) and girls (Gargi & Maitreyi) within the campus boundary, offering balanced diets and recreation centers.

  • Capacity: 1000+ student accommodations
  • Services: Wi-Fi, common TV rooms, visitor lounges
  • Security: 24/7 guard surveillance and biometric entry

Sports & Gym Complex

Encouraging students to lead active lives, CUSB hosts multi-sport fields, athletics tracks, and outdoor open gyms alongside indoor courts for table tennis and chess.

  • Outdoor: Cricket field, football pitch, volleyball, badminton
  • Indoor: Fully equipped weight training and fitness gym
  • Annual Event: CUSB Khel Mahotsav
Explore Sports & Gym

Central Instruments Facility (CIF)

Equipped with top-tier tools (XRD, SEM, FTIR, NMR) to support advanced research scholars in biological sciences, physics, chemistry, and environmental science domains.

  • Aim: Foster high-quality research publications
  • Access: Available to internal students and external institutions
  • Lab standards: NABL aligned practices

Cafeteria & Mess

Spacious canteen serving hygienic food, beverages, and snacks at government approved subsidized rates. Hosts food options matching culinary diversities of students.

  • Quality: FSSAI certified food inspection
  • Capacity: Accommodates 150+ students in dining hall
  • Menu: North Indian, South Indian, fast-food, juices

Health & Medical Center

On-campus health clinic providing basic medical assistance, emergency triage, pharmacy services, and routine checkups for students and faculty 24/7.

  • Staff: Full-time resident doctors and nursing assistants
  • Triage: Dedicated on-campus ambulance service
  • Coverage: Health cards issued for all registered students
