University Legal Cell

Providing expert legal opinions, statutory vetting, compliance reviews, and litigation advisory for the University administration.

About the Legal Cell

The Legal Cell of the Central University of South Bihar is constituted with the primary objective of ensuring smooth, hassle-free administrative functioning without legal obstruction. It conducts rigorous legal vetting for internal and external affairs such as litigation, investigations, statutory compliances, and dispute resolutions.

The Cell provides legal opinions, reviews institutional contracts, MOUs, service agreements, and tenders; assists in drafting executive resolutions, notifications, and caveats; and actively supports balanced decision-making across all university statutory bodies.
