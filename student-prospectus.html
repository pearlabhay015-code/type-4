Prospectus

Every year, before commencement of examination and admission process, university releases new prospectus. Attempts are made to provide a hard copy of prospectus to all students admitted. Additionally, students can access the copy of prospectus and download from the following links. Rules and regulations described in the prospectus, published in a year-X, applies to the students admitted in that year-X during the entire course of of their program until completion.
